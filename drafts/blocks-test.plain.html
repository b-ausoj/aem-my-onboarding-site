Frequently asked

Is wild camping legal in Switzerland?

There is no single national law. A single-night bivouac above the treeline is broadly tolerated; pitching a tent in valleys, forests or farmland generally is not.

Can I light a fire while wild camping?

Usually no — use a stove and respect fire bans, which are common in dry spells.

Is wild camping allowed in the Swiss National Park?

No. All camping and bivouacking is forbidden throughout the National Park.
